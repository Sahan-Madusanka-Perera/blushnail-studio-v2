# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite (vanilla HTML/CSS/JS) + GSAP/ScrollTrigger + Lenis, deployed to Vercel. Second concept for the same pitch ("The Sample-Tip Board"), alongside the Velvet Vitrine in ../blush-nail-studio-site. Bespoke, image-led single page that must load fast from an Instagram DM link.

## Users

Primary: women in Sydney's inner south-west (Bexley North, Bexley, Kingsgrove, Earlwood, Rockdale, Arncliffe and surrounds) looking for a nail artist for gel, acrylic or detailed nail art, mostly on a phone, often arriving from Instagram or Google Maps. They are choosing someone they trust with a 1–2 hour appointment and a look they will wear for weeks.
Secondary (for this pitch): Genevieve, the owner, who must look at it and think "that's my work, presented better than I ever have".

## Product Purpose

A landing page for Blush Nail Studio ("Nails by Genevieve"), a solo nail artist's studio at 2A Sarsfield Cct, Bexley North NSW 2207. Its job: show the range and skill of her hand-painted work, make the visitor want her hands on their nails, and get them into an Instagram DM to book. Built by Seqlith as an unsolicited concept to win the studio as a client.

## Positioning

One artist, one chair: every set is done by Genevieve herself, mostly freehand. Her feed shows unusual range for a single tech: soft blush ombré and milky French; 3D gel flowers, bows and charms; chrome, cat-eye and aurora finishes; gem-encrusted stilettos; tortoiseshell; marble; hand-painted characters, florals, snowflakes and Lunar New Year sets. Long almond, stiletto and coffin shapes dominate.

## Operating Context

- Hours (IG bio): bookings open 7 days, 8am–8pm.
- Booking: by Instagram DM only (@blushnail.studio). Primary CTA = open DM.
- Services named in bio: Gel Nails, Acrylic, Nail Art. Hashtags add Gel-X, 3D nail art, chrome, cat-eye, French, hand-painted.
- Studio is private/appointment-based (residential address); show suburb and map link, not walk-in language.
- She occasionally closes for travel and announces it on IG.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Primary CTA: "Book via Instagram DM" → https://ig.me/m/blushnail.studio (fallback profile link).
- No prices (user decision). No phone, email, website or booking system exists.
- Small footer credit "Concept by Seqlith" linking https://www.seqlith.com/.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: "Blush Nail Studio"; IG display name "Nails by Genevieve"; handle @blushnail.studio.
- Bio line: "Based in Bexley North, Sydney, Australia 💅🏻 Bookings open now 7 days 8am-8pm. DM for bookings."
- No logo asset exists beyond the IG avatar.

## Evidence on Hand

- ~85 owner Instagram nail photos (2023–2026), 1440×1800 and 3024×4032: `_raw/ig/`. Preferred imagery. Personal wedding photos and a travel-closure notice in the export are excluded and must never be used.
- Google Business profile: 40 photos (17 owner, 23 customer), most recent 2026-11-16. No rating, review count or review text was provided: do not fabricate testimonials or star ratings.
- Hero/editorial mood imagery may use licensed stock (Unsplash) of a model's hands; it must be labelled as a placeholder to replace with Genevieve's own shoot before production.

## Product Principles

1. Her work is the hero: real sets, large, close, sharp.
2. Booking is one tap from anywhere on the page.
3. Feel like a boutique atelier, never a strip-mall salon menu.
4. Say only what is true: no invented prices, reviews or awards.
