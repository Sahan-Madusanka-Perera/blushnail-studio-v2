    What she does

    No price list here. Every set is quoted to your design: send Genevieve your inspo by DM and she'll reply with the price and a time.

    1. Gel

      Glossy gel colour and builder gel, from clean milky nudes to full art. Cat-eye, chrome and aurora finishes on request.

      Ask for a quote

    2. Acrylic

      Sculpted extensions for length and strength, filed to the shape you want and ready for colour or art.

      Ask for a quote

    3. Gel-X

      Soft-gel extensions for a light, natural-feeling set with real length.

      Ask for a quote

    4. Nail art

      Freehand painting, 3D gel flowers and bows, charms, crystals, French and ombré, marble, tortoiseshell and seasonal sets.

      Ask for a quote

    Booking takes one DM

    1. 01 Pick a tip

      Choose a set from the board, or bring your own inspo photo.

    2. 02 Send a DM

      Message @blushnail.studio with the set, the length you want and the days that suit you.

    3. 03 Get a time

      Genevieve replies with the price and an appointment, any day between 8am and 8pm.

    4. 04 Wear it out

      Leave with a set painted for your hands only, then tag @blushnail.studio.
